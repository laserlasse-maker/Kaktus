Who we are, and what it means to be a Needle.

01

Overview

Kaktus, international, tight-knit and friendly alliance.

We bend, but never break. And watch out when we spring back, our needles sting.

All are welcome, as long as you are friendly and you have fun.

02

The Needles

Our alliance is Kaktus, and our members are Needles. We have few rules, our main goal is to grow stronger together, enjoy the game and have fun while doing so. We also like to win and punch way above our weight in the different events. But never at the cost of having fun.

03

Rules

There are not many rules in Kaktus, we don't need or want them. But please observe these:

  1. Have fun and be kind to others, both members and fellow state 505 survivors.
  2. Do not attack trucks from 505. Set "exclude cars from this state" in Doomsday Express.
  3. Read markers and messages from R4.
Most importantly Have fun! Join the chat, share jokes, be part of it. Or just hang around quietly. Both are fine.
