Tip of the Day Series

General Hints

Here you'll find a list of tips and tricks that you might find useful. These are also shared in the "Tip of the day" chat.

01

Sharing a Location

Use this to share a settlement, an enemy, or a Gene Clash target with the alliance.

Click the item you want to share. A window opens with two icons at the top: three dots, and a star.

Three dots. Choose a chat. This shares a shortcut. Others in that chat can click it to jump straight to the item.

Star. Creates a bookmark. Click the orange star at the top of your screen to return to it later.

Use the three dots when you are under attack and need to share an enemy location fast, or when a fatty is heading your way. Use the star to save alliance or rival locations you want to find again later.

Three dots shares now, the star saves for later.

02

Garrisons & Hospital

Understanding the troop cap lets you grow your army faster than the garrison alone allows.

Garrison level sets your maximum troop count. At max, you cannot train more troops. Troops in the hospital do not count toward this cap. If you are at max troops and you heal hospital troops, your total troops can exceed the cap. This raises your power and strengthens your rallies.

"Overtrain" by keeping troops in the hospital while you recruit up to your max garrison. Once your garrison is full, start healing troops to push past the cap. Combine this with alliance points: train on Fridays, heal on Saturdays, for a bigger point and strength boost.

Hospital troops don't count toward your cap, so heal after you're full to grow past it.

03

Constructing

There is no exact formula for build order, but these principles hold.

Core buildings are the power plant (PP), barracks #1, and the garrison. Supporting these are PP requirements like the lab and comms outpost. The golden aegis and two other golden statues limit your PP level. Season buildings (dragon) set attack limits and should be upgraded for resistance. Resource buildings (kitchen, smelter, lumber yard, refinery) keep materials flowing.

Progress is gated by resources and time. Speedups solve time. Production and gathering solve resources.

Strategy depends on your level. Below PP 25, keep all buildings, including production, equal to your power plant level. Above PP 25, resource buildings give diminishing returns, so focus on PP and barracks until maxed, then address the rest as needed.

To maximise score, time your speedup use with Turbo Turtle, Power Play, and the Alliance Duel. If possible, hold off unwrapping finished buildings until the Alliance Duel gives points for them.

Prioritise PP and barracks below level 25, then shift focus, and time your speedups to scoring events.

04

The Alliance Duel (AD)

AD is a weekly event where we're matched against another alliance and compete for points across categories.

Click the VS icon in the right menu to see the day's topic, your score, and the weekly ranking. VS is the Alliance Duel. The event name gets translated differently across languages, but the icon itself always shows "VS." To unlock all 9 reward chests, fully research the personal "Alliance Technology."

Combine AD with Power Play (Big Game) and Turbo Turtle so you score points in every event at once. R4 will post a reminder. AD is not mandatory, but Kaktus aims to win every week.

Stack AD with Power Play and Turbo Turtle to score everywhere at once.

05

The Reservoir Raid (RR)

RR is a weekly map event where two alliances fight for control of water-producing buildings.

There are 7 water-producing buildings and 4 bonus buildings. Water towers appear after 30 minutes. Throwing a player out of a building spills some of their collected water. Picking up spilled water counts as our gain and their loss. See the "info" section for building details.

The two alliances start on opposite sides and fight for building control. It is an open fight, settlements included. Damaged troops heal when the event ends. Some relocators are given for the event, and you may also use your own.

Strategy depends on the opponent. Our usual approach:

  • Most players are assigned to buildings, alone or in teams.
  • Stronger players assist first, then focus on the central reservoir.
  • When towers appear, we act opportunistically.

Everyone has a task, and stronger players pivot to the central reservoir once buildings are covered.

06

Gathering Bonus & Free Peace Flare

Two small menu features that most players under-use.

Near the top of your screen, there is a small "up" arrow above a small curve. Pressing this opens a menu with your bonuses.

  • Development → Gathering speed. Buy the "mega gathering boost" for 600 gems. It increases gathering speed by 100% for 24 hours.
  • Combat → Peace flare. You have 5 free, 8-hour peace flares. These recharge over time.

Use the gathering boost daily to speed up resource collection. Use a peace flare if you think your settlement is at risk of attack.

Buy the gathering boost daily, and use a free peace flare when you're at risk.

07

Dire Dispatch (DD) & Doomsday Express (DE)

Two daily events where you send out parties and trucks to gather supplies and score points.

DE ranks trucks from A (best) to D (worst). DD ranks missions from S (best) via A to B (worst). You start with a random rank and can use tickets to re-roll. After 5 re-rolls, you always get the best option. In DD, you can also assist alliance members to shorten their time.

When these are scored in the Alliance Duel, only A trucks and S missions count. Go for the highest rank whenever possible.

You can also raid opponents, from our own state or others, to steal supplies. Raiding is optional. If you raid trucks, filter out trucks from 505. You will be punished for raiding trucks from your own state.

For optimal growth, do both missions daily at the highest rank you can reach.

Re-roll toward the top rank every day, and never raid your own state's trucks.

08

The Ghoul Lion Hunt

Two objectives: an individual score and an alliance score, both earned by launching and joining rallies against the arena.

  • Individual (I) ranking. Max reward at 600 million score.
  • Alliance (A) ranking. Target is 8 billion total for the alliance reward.

Check the score during the event by clicking the arena. R4 will announce when the alliance target is reached.

When the arena opens, immediately start a rally with your strongest march, and use your other marches to join other rallies. Once both objectives are reached, only start rallies with your strongest march, and stop joining others. This lets absent members keep earning points through automation, which helps both the alliance and everyone's individual progress.

Enable "auto join" for the lion hunt if you can't join live. Some players may be asked to run a second wave, to spread out rallies and use the full event window.

We're trialling this approach for a few hunts to see how it works. (Credit: King Louis, who wrote up this strategy.) Go all-in early, then switch to solo strongest-march rallies once both targets are hit.

09

Rallies, Resistance & Strength

Two stats decide your success in combat on the main map.

  • Unit strength. Driven by hero development, equipment, and troops. Determines your damage output.
  • Draconic Resistance. Driven by dragon development. Too low, and your damage is reduced by up to 98%. This season's name for the generic Resistance stat, see Vocabulary for the full mapping across seasons.

There are two ways to attack enemies:

  • Attack. A single round of combat against one enemy.
  • Rally. A group attack against a stronger enemy. One player starts it, other players join, one march each. Up to 5 rounds, and the party must win all of them.

There are three enemy types (this guide's names, season-specific naming for the first two handled separately):

  • Infected (1-30). Solo, single attack. Your own strength and resistance decide the outcome.
  • Infected fiends (1-10). Group, single rally. Combined strength and resistance decide the outcome.
  • Roaming infected (6-10). Group, multiple rallies. Combined strength decides the outcome.

The highest resistance in your group sets the total resistance, so stronger players carry the group further. In practice: fiends up to level 5 are safe. With good joins, level 7 is reachable. Roamers up to level 8 are generally safe; with a strong player joining, level 9-10 is possible.

Strength decides damage, resistance decides whether it lands, and your strongest player sets the ceiling for the group.

10

Honour Expeditions

Honour expeditions give rewards and points for a special store.

There are 3 types, each tied to a hero faction:

Mountain Citadel (MC)
Stalwart / Green
Howling Sands (HS)
Rover / Yellow
Azure Apex (AA)
Aeronaut / Light blue

AA gives the best rewards, followed by HS, then MC. Each expedition has 20 battles of increasing difficulty. The matching hero faction gives a bonus, but overall power matters most. A hero sent on one expedition is locked out of the others until the event resets, every 2-3 days.

Mariner / Blue is a fourth faction that has no dedicated expedition. A Mariner hero is a wildcard: it can act as any faction when paired with other heroes, so it can still support whichever expedition you send it on.

Aim to clear all 20 levels for full rewards. Put your strongest heroes on the hardest mission you can clear, then fill out the rest. Equipment isn't locked, only heroes are. If you're close to finishing a mission, temporarily move armour from your main army onto the heroes assigned to it, push it through, then move the armour back.

Match faction to expedition where you can, but send your strongest heroes to the hardest mission you can clear.

11

Roamers

Roamers are a special type of infected that move around the map, level 6 to 10, and get more dangerous at higher levels.

Roamers must be attacked by rallies with more than one member. They start at 100% health. Each successful rally removes 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, or 100% of their health. Unsuccessful rallies remove nothing, so it may take multiple rallies to kill one.

If you fail to kill a roamer, it walks toward your settlement. Unless killed, it will attack your settlement, burn your barricade, and put troops in the hospital, or worse. If you can't kill it, relocate until the danger passes.

The first roamer killed each day gives the biggest rewards, so aim for the highest level you can manage first. If strong players are around, ask first, then take a level 10. Always go for level 8 or higher unless no strong players are available. Later kills that day give resources, and higher levels give more.

Kill your first roamer of the day as high-level as possible, then keep going higher for resources.

12

Helping Hands

Ways every member can help each other grow stronger.

  • Aid, asking. Need help with research, construction, or healing time? Use "timer help," or the handshake icon over your building.
  • Aid, giving. Click the handshake icon in the alliance tab to give another member a timer boost. Inactive members help too, passively, just slower.
  • Rallies. Join rallies started by others. You contribute strength and resistance, and earn rewards.
  • Auto join. Enable it under Alliance → Auto join, so you join others' rallies even while offline. Keep your strongest army free, not gathering, so it's ready to join.
  • Chat. Keep it alive if you're feeling social. It builds a friendly alliance and creates bonds.

Heal in small increments, 1k at a time, in the hospital. Aid from others heals these fast, and for free.

Give timer aid, join rallies, and heal in small batches so others can speed you up for free.

13

The Alliance Siege (AS)

A daily event where Kaktus is matched against one other alliance, one attack per person against one enemy.

Each person gets one attack against one enemy, and each enemy can only be attacked by one person. The opposing alliance's members are ranked by strength. The higher the rank you attack, the higher the score, for both you and Kaktus.

Attack the highest-ranking enemy you can beat, for maximum score. If you're not strong enough to take on any opponent, attack the bottom-ranked one instead. This guarantees at least one point.

Punch as high as you can, and take the guaranteed bottom point if you can't.

R3 and above are active, R1 stays unless the alliance needs the space, and R4 titles mark responsibility, not extra power.

15

Precision Blueprints

Blueprints are key items for upgrading your Chief collection past the green grade.

Chief collection items move through grades: white, green, blue, purple, gold. Level 1 of each item uses only general parts, which you'll collect plenty of through normal play. Higher levels need precision blueprints: 10 blueprints for each level within green, 100 blueprints to promote an item from green to blue, then 30 blueprints for each level within blue. Blueprints are generally not available in stores.

Ways to get them:

  • First daily kill of a level 8, 9, or 10 roamer
  • The weekly free "crates of choice" bundle
  • Purchase in the reservoir store once signature gear tokens are sold out (prioritise gear tokens first)
  • Rewards from events like Push and Play, Chamber of Secrets

Prioritise blueprints above speedups or other items you can get elsewhere.

Blueprints are scarce and mostly earned, not bought, so grab every source you can.

16

Active & Indirect Help

Your rallies help more than just yourself, especially now that Kaktus has over 60 members.

When you run your own rallies against infected, you create chests for yourself, for those who join, and for all other Needles in Kaktus. Each chest is small, but they add up at scale.

Run as many rallies as you can during the day to help others grow. When you're not playing, enable Auto join under Alliance → Auto join → Edit, so you fill in others' rallies automatically. If you're not strong, you still earn rewards. If you're strong, your resistance and strength help others. Enable auto join for the Ghoul Lion Hunt too if you can't join live; toward the end of the hunt, space is saved for auto-joiners.

Every rally you run creates chests for the whole alliance, so run as many as you can and leave auto join on.

17

Increasing Darkness Resistance

This season's fights require Darkness Resistance, this season's name for the generic Resistance stat (see Vocabulary), mainly built through the lighthouse.

Without resistance, damage against Noctis Acolytes, Noctis Rex, and Bastion Infected is reduced by up to 99%. The lighthouse and its associated Tungsten and Quartz plants build this resistance. Smaller buildings in front of the lighthouse (cooling tower, heat tower, observation post, solar panel) can wait until the lighthouse itself is built.

Building the lighthouse unlocks skills. The "Worm trap" skill, at the bottom of the tree, will be needed next week.

Prioritise reaching Worm Trap over maxing every skill along the way. Kaktus never encourages spending money, but if you choose to, the Season Pass and Season Weekly Pass unlock additional Tungsten and Quartz plants to speed up construction.

Rush the lighthouse to Worm Trap before maxing anything else, and skip the small buildings until it's done.
