The Clash

Mechanics for the Clash. This is based on the "Gene Clash" event. We expect the coming "Copper Clash" is similar. But we'll update this as we learn more. This is tips on team strategy, and scoring, for every Needle competing on the season map.

01

Overview

Finalized

The first rule of the Gene Clash is: don't waste anchors!

The Gene Clash is a game of territorial conquest on a separate map, available from the 'season' menu. We compete with 7 other alliances.

The goal is to score DNA points by occupying buildings on the map. Points are awarded to individuals (Merit) and the alliance (DNA). DNA points count towards the overall season score.

Every player gets 8 anchors per day, 10 if you have bought a pass. You can store up to 15 anchors. Anchors are used to attack and to garrison buildings.

There are two types of buildings: Lairs and Workbenches. Lairs can be attacked, and the alliance with the highest score at midnight wins. Workbenches must be garrisoned, and the alliance with the most score from occupation during the day wins the Workbench.

New buildings open every day. If no opponent can reach a Workbench, one garrison is enough to win, no need to send more.

Markers will be placed to guide garrisons and attacks. Group messages will follow with details.

02

Strategy & Groups

Finalized

Kaktus wants to do well in the Gene Clash, for our members, for the alliance, and for 505. To achieve this, we have split our members into two groups, based on knowledge of our opposition and the strength of your first march.

Team A will focus on scoring individual and alliance points by taking Lairs. These do not require garrisons, and will not expose your troops to costly PvP combat. We will use markers to guide which lairs can be attacked. Your effort helps us build bonuses.

Team B (stronger players) will focus on Workbenches and territorial control. This means attacking and garrisoning Workbenches.

A Workbench has 2, 3 or 4 occupation slots. Once 50% of the day's total points is reached, the Workbench is won, see Scoring for the exact numbers. No need to spend more anchors once that point is hit.

Where to focus depends on how the map evolves and what the opposition does. Markers and chats will guide you.

If you're awake at the daily reset, focus on taking and garrisoning Workbenches quickly, to start scoring early.

03

Scoring

Finalized

Lairs and Workbenches score differently.

Lairs are simple: points are scored per attack, summed up at the end of the day, and the alliance with the most points wins.

Workbenches score by occupation time. Each has 2, 3 or 4 slots where troops can be garrisoned. Every minute an alliance holds a slot gives either 1 or 2 occupation points, no matter what bonus the alliance has. The alliance with the highest score at the end of the day wins the Workbench.

Since the score per minute is fixed, there is a maximum score per Workbench size. 50% of this secures a win.

SizeWin Threshold (50%)
S2,161

M and L have the same slot setup (2 slots at 1 point, 1 slot at 2 points), so they share the same threshold. Same for XL and XXL (2 slots at 1 point, 2 slots at 2 points).

Once we hit this score, we have won, stop spending anchors on it.
