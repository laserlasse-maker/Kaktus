Copper Clash Prep

A working prep guide, built ahead of launch. This may replace The Clash once Copper Clash goes live, for now they sit side by side.

Status This event hasn't opened on our server yet. Everything below is our best working read of it, some of it confirmed, some of it a working assumption. Where we're not sure, we say so.
01

What It Is

Copper Clash starts Season 3, Week 2. Only the top 8 alliances on the server compete. Two factions face off. Alliances get grouped into a faction. The winning faction gets upgraded season rewards.

Faction names, per the game's own Season III guide: Blade of Light vs Night Reaper. A separate outside source used "Scythe of Night" instead, we're treating Night Reaper as correct since it's confirmed by two independent sources.

02

Our Position, Working Assumption

No official metric found in-game for the top 8 cutoff, only a note that it exists. We assume it uses overall server power rank, since that is the only ranking visible anywhere in the client.

Kaktus is rank 8 on power. Gap to rank 9 is 1.33B, stable over time. Gap to rank 7 is 4.86B, not close to the upper bracket. This is unconfirmed until Copper Clash opens in Week 2.

A separate copper collected leaderboard exists (rank 5 on 505, rank 13 in faction), but that tracks passive city income, not Copper Clash eligibility, don't confuse the two.

03

Copper Sources

Lairs
Fast attacks, good for quick points.
Mines
Including XXL mines. Need faction coordination to hold.
Merit
Earned through combat and skirmishes, personal solo points.

Focus on all three, not just one.

04

How Marching Works

Action points limit your attacks, plan targets, don't spam. Territory links extend your attack range, link territory early. After day 5, you can strike from faction core territory, even without personal tiles connected there.

05

How Matching Works

Each Copper Clash map pairs 6 alliances total, 3 from each faction side. You are matched against the opposing faction, not random servers. Rankings are tracked at faction level, not just alliance level, your attacks contribute to a shared faction score.

06

Map Points

Locations start at 200 points. Select high-value nodes start at 300 points. This is part of why Lairs and Mines differ in value, not just in how hard they are to capture.

07

Action Checklist Before Week 2

  • Confirm eligibility metric the moment Copper Clash opens.
  • Link territory early.
  • Assign mine defense roles now.
  • Save an action point usage plan.
  • Watch rank 9 for sudden power spikes.
