Intro

Finalized

Welcome to the Tip of the Day series "Growing Stronger!"

This series gives tips on how to grow stronger. It is for players with a power plant below level 30. At level 30, your strategy changes. You will need more than one strong march. We cover this in a later series.

This series follows one rule: make your first march as strong as possible. Give your other marches what remains. This series does not cover total battle power. That topic has its own series.

Over the next days, we cover these topics:

  • Heroes: Tiers, Class and faction
  • Heroes: Stars, skills and experience
  • Heroes: Armour, upgrading, enhancing and reforging
  • Heroes: Weapons, obtaining and reforging
  • March: Strength and composition
  • The importance of troops
  • Chief collection

These tips are not fixed rules. They come from our own experience and from other players. If you disagree, or have other suggestions, let's discuss.

01

Heroes Explained 1: Tiers, Class & Faction

Finalized

A hero has many characteristics. Today we explain the basics.

Tier. A hero has one of 3 tiers: R, SR or SSR.

R is the basic tier. Example heroes: Rusty, Ghost. R heroes have a blue portrait background. R heroes have weak skills, stats and weapons. R heroes cannot evolve. Use R heroes only if you have no other heroes. Replace R heroes as soon as possible.

SR is the middle tier. SR heroes are stronger than R heroes. SR heroes have better skills and stats. SR heroes cannot equip weapons. Some SR heroes can evolve to SSR. Example heroes: Chef, Sarge. SR heroes should form the backbone of your march early on. Replace SR heroes with SSR heroes over time.

SSR is the elite tier. SSR heroes have the best stats and skills. SSR heroes can equip special weapons. Replace your R and SR heroes with SSR heroes as you grow stronger.

Class. A hero has one of 3 classes: Guard (Shield), Gunner (Pistol) or Marksman (Rifle). The class decides which troops the hero can lead. The class also decides the bonus from reforged armour.

Faction. A hero has one of 4 factions: Stalwart (green shield), Aeronaut (light cloud), Rover (yellow) or Mariner (blue wave). The faction gives bonuses against specific enemies. The mix of factions in a march gives extra bonuses, explained in a later tip. Mariner heroes can act as any of the other 3 factions.

Aim for 5 SSR heroes in your first march. Use only one faction. Mix all 3 classes. If your first march is mostly R and SR heroes, upgrade this first.

02

Heroes Explained 2: Stars, Skills & Experience

Finalized

Heroes grow through 3 resources: stars, skills and level. These resources are limited. Use them with care on the heroes in your first march.

Stars. A hero has 0 to 10 stars. Each star has 6 levels. Each full star increases stats like Attack, Defence and Health. Each full star also raises the maximum skill level. At specific star levels, like 8 and 10, all heroes in the march get a bonus.

Stars are raised using shards. Shards are specific to one hero, or generic to one tier (R, SR, SSR). Generic shards can convert to specific shards.

Skills. Skills are unique to each hero. A skill can boost the hero itself, for example a higher attack stat. A skill can boost all heroes in the march, for example restoring health. A skill can also be global, for example lowering the energy cost of rallies and attacks.

Higher tier heroes have more and better skills. SSR heroes have one special skill. This skill boosts all stats of the hero. It unlocks once all other skills reach level 30.

Level. Level defines how many escorts and troops a hero brings. Level increases through experience. The maximum level is capped by your power plant level.

Locking rules. Stars and skills are locked to one hero once used. Experience can move freely between heroes.

How to prioritize. Focus stars, skills and experience on your first march. Do not spread them across many heroes.

Most players start with a few 10-star R heroes. As you get SSR heroes, replace an R hero with an SSR hero of the same faction and class. Example: replace Rusty with Nikola. Then collect shards specific to Nikola. Convert generic yellow shards to Nikola shards to raise his stars.

Aim for 10 stars on your first march heroes over time. Aim for 8 stars first, since this usually gives a march-wide bonus.

Focus skill growth on your first march heroes. Exception: global skills. Example: Lucky's skill "Wild Hunt" reduces stamina cost for all marches. Upgrade global skills even on heroes outside your first march.

Pick the heroes for your first march. Prioritize them. Focus stars, skills and experience on these heroes to raise the total power of your first march.
