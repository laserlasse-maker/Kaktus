Terminology Reference

Vocabulary

Shared vocabulary for every Kaktus Tip of the Day series. Check new drafts against this list before publishing. Update here first, let the series follow.

01

Core Structure

Faction gives bonuses against specific enemies, and the mix of factions across a march gives extra bonuses.

Chief
The player's own avatar. Not a hero. Has its own equipment set, the Chief collection.
Hero
Individual character with equipment, armour, skills, stars, experience, faction, class and tier. Supported by troops.
Tier
R, SR, SSR for heroes. Chief collection also has tiers, a separate meaning, context decides which. See Open Items.
Class
Guard, Gunner, Marksman.
Faction
Stalwart/Green, Rover/Yellow, Aeronaut/Light blue, Mariner/Blue.
Mariner
Wildcard faction. No dedicated Honour Expedition. A Mariner hero can act as any faction when paired with other heroes.
March
1–5 heroes combined. A player has 1–6 marches.
Troops
Support a march, add to total power.
Escorts
Brought by a hero, count scales with hero level.
Power Plant Level
Main player progression metric, level 1–30. Shorthand: PP.
02

Main Map

Attack
A single round of combat against one enemy.
Rally
A group attack against a stronger enemy. One player starts it, others join, one march each. Up to 5 rounds, the party must win all of them.
Draconic Resistance
Too low, and damage is reduced by up to 98%.

03

Garrison & Hospital

Garrison Level
Sets the troop cap.
Hospital
Troops here do not count toward the cap. Healing them after reaching cap pushes total troops above cap.
04

Gene Clash

Anchors
8 per day, 10 with a purchased pass. Max storage 15. Used to attack and to garrison.
Lairs
Cannot be garrisoned, only attacked. Scored per attack, summed at day's end.
Workbenches
Must be garrisoned. Score by occupation time, 1 or 2 points per minute per slot.
Building
Shared term when the Lair vs Workbench distinction is not needed.
DNA Points
Alliance score, counts toward season score.
Merit
Individual score.

05

Honour Expeditions

MC — Mountain Citadel
Stalwart / Green
HS — Howling Sands
Rover / Yellow
AA — Azure Apex
Aeronaut / Light blue
  • Reward order: AA best, then HS, then MC.
  • 20 battles per expedition, increasing difficulty.
  • A hero sent on one expedition is locked out of the others until reset, 2–3 days.
  • Equipment is not locked, only heroes. Can be moved between heroes to finish a mission, then moved back.
  • Mariner has no dedicated expedition, it's a wildcard faction, see Core Structure.
06

Darkness Resistance

Seasonal mechanic, current season.

  • Needed to fight Noctis Acolytes, Noctis Rex, Bastion Infected. Without it, damage drops up to 99%.
  • Obtained mainly by building the lighthouse, alongside Tungsten and Quartz plants.
  • Smaller buildings (cooling tower, heat tower, observation post, solar panel) can wait until the lighthouse is built.
  • Skill priority: reach Worm Trap rather than maxing every skill along the way.
  • Optional purchases: Season Pass, Season Weekly Pass, both unlock an extra Tungsten and Quartz plant.
07

Abbreviations

PP
Conflicting: Power Plant and Power Play, two separate events/concepts. Unresolved, see Open Items.
AD
Alliance Duel. Same event as "VS", the untranslated icon label. Weekly, category-based scoring against a matched alliance.
TT
Turbo Turtle. Daily event, points scored by performing specific actions on specific days, e.g. construction speedups on Tuesdays.
Power Play
A separate daily event, points scored by specific actions on specific days and timeslots. Sometimes overlaps with TT (e.g. Tuesdays 02:00–06:00 CEST both give a construction bonus), sometimes doesn't. Also abbreviated "PP", see conflict above.
DD
Dire Dispatch. Daily mission event, ranked S (best) via A to B (worst).
DE
Doomsday Express. Daily truck event, ranked A (best) to D (worst).
RR
Reservoir Raid. Weekly map event, two alliances fight for control of water-producing buildings.
AS
Alliance Siege. Daily event, one attack per person against a matched alliance's ranked members.
